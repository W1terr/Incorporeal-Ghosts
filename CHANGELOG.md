# Changelog

## 1.0

First release.

- Ghosts and Wispmothers are incorporeal: weapons pass through them and damaging magic, shouts and enchantments do
  nothing, for every attacker (you, followers, enemies), also with Precision / Valhalla Combat
- Ghost weapons hurt them anywhere: silver weapons, Ghostblade, Dawnbreaker, bound weapons, Turn Undead / undead bane
  enchantments
- The Witcher Signs' Yrden makes them vulnerable: inside the sign they take physical form (their ghost shader comes off
  and they turn solid) and every attack hurts them; outside it they are ghosts again
- Data-driven detection (keywords, races, ghost abilities found by their effect shader) with compatibility files
- Spectral sound when an attack passes through
- SKSE Menu Framework settings and status page, in the 9 languages Skyrim ships with
- Skyrim SE 1.5.97 and AE (1.6.x / 1.7.x)
